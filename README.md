# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

OctoAcme uses a simple, repeatable project management model that moves work from idea to delivery through initiation, planning, execution, release, and continuous improvement. The goal is to align stakeholders early, define measurable success, and keep teams focused on delivering small, testable increments with clear ownership and accountability. The process documents in this repository standardize the way OctoAcme plans work, tracks progress, manages risks, communicates status, and learns from each release or milestone.

At the start of a project, teams validate the business need, identify stakeholders and champions, and define a lightweight one-pager with the problem statement, goals, success metrics, timeline, and risks. If the initiative is approved, the team moves into planning: they create a prioritized backlog, estimate scope, agree on milestones and release timing, and document the Definition of Done and QA approach. During execution, work is tracked with a project board, daily standups, and regular delivery reviews. PRs are expected to be small, include issue links and acceptance criteria, and pass CI checks and at least one review before merge.

Project success depends on clear roles and communication. Product Managers define the problem and prioritize value; Project Managers coordinate delivery, risk, and communication; Developers build, test, and document; QA validates acceptance criteria; and stakeholders provide input and approvals. The organization emphasizes transparency through ongoing status checks, weekly PM/PdM alignment, stakeholder updates, and escalation paths that move from the team to the PM, Product Lead, and Sponsor when needed. Risk management is handled through a risk register and active monitoring, with cross-team dependencies highlighted in the project board and escalated as needed.

Quality assurance is built into every phase. Teams use unit tests, integration tests, and end-to-end smoke tests for critical flows; security scans run in CI; and manual QA is used when required for feature acceptance. Before release, teams confirm acceptance criteria have been met, complete smoke tests, draft release notes, and document rollback or mitigation plans. After each sprint or milestone, OctoAcme captures learning through a retrospective, records action items with owners and deadlines, and continues improving the process based on evidence and team feedback.
